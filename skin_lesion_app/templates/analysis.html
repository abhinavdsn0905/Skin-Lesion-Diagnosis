Plain Language Summary

Understanding The Research Report in Plain English

A simple breakdown of our findings designed for everyday users without medical or data-science backgrounds.

1

Why is classification difficult?

Harmless moles (Melanocytic nevi) make up 67% of skin spots, while dangerous Melanoma is only ~11%. Because AI trains mostly on normal moles, it defaults to "harmless" unless carefully calibrated for rare cancers.

2

Which AI model worked best?

ResNet-50 led with 82.42% accuracy. EfficientNet-B0 was close at 81.33% while being smaller and faster. DINOv2 reached 75.82% — promising but not yet ahead of CNNs.

3

Can AI catch melanoma?

Models correctly identified melanoma 49–57% of the time, meaning ~4 in 10 were missed. Key takeaway: AI is a useful second opinion, but a dermatologist with a dermoscope remains essential.

4

What do risk colors mean?

🔴 Red (High Risk) — Melanoma, BCC, Actinic Keratoses.
🟡 Yellow (Medium Risk) — Vascular lesions; check if changing.
🟢 Green (Low Risk) — Benign moles; non-cancerous.
