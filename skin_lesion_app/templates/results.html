💡 Key Findings

🥇

ResNet-50 Leads on Accuracy

ResNet-50 achieved the highest test accuracy (82.42%) and strong per-class F1 scores, benefiting from 8 epochs of fine-tuning with AdamW and class-weighted cross-entropy loss.

📡

EfficientNet Best AUROC

EfficientNet-B0 achieved the top Macro AUROC (95.70%) in just 5 epochs, demonstrating excellent discriminative ability across all seven classes despite slightly lower accuracy.

🧊

DINOv2 Frozen Lags Behind

Using frozen DINOv2 features with logistic regression yields 73.93% accuracy. Partial fine-tuning (last 2 transformer blocks) improves this to 75.82% — still below CNN baselines.

⚖️

Class Imbalance Challenge

The ~58:1 imbalance (nv vs df) makes balanced accuracy and macro-F1 more informative than raw accuracy. All models struggle with rare classes like df and akiec.

🔬

AUROC vs F1 Trade-off

High AUROC (91–96%) across all models shows strong class-separability. Lower Macro-F1 (58–65%) reflects difficulty making confident predictions on minority classes.

🚀

Future Work

Consistent checkpoint selection, external validation sets, calibrated confidence scores, and dermatologist-annotated segmentation masks are recommended next steps.

⚙️ Training Configuration

ResNet-50

  • Pretrained on ImageNet
  • 8 epochs fine-tuning
  • AdamW optimizer
  • Class-weighted CE loss
  • LR scheduler: StepLR
  • Input: 224×224 RGB

EfficientNet-B0

  • Pretrained on ImageNet
  • 5 epochs fine-tuning
  • AdamW optimizer
  • Class-weighted CE loss
  • LR scheduler: CosineAnnealing
  • Input: 224×224 RGB

DINOv2 (facebook/dinov2-base)

  • Frozen backbone → logistic regression
  • Partial fine-tuning: last 2 transformer blocks
  • 3 epochs for fine-tuning
  • AdamW with low LR for backbone
  • CLS token output → linear head
  • Input: 224×224 RGB
